Create Your Switch

Deploy a new dead man's switch contract. Set your beneficiary and timeout period.

If you don't check in within this period, the beneficiary can claim.

Why it matters

security

Non-custodial

Your funds never leave your control until your beneficiary claims. No middleman.

schedule

Time-locked

Set your own check-in interval. Miss it, and your legacy activates automatically.

verified

Onchain forever

Deployed on Monad. No server downtime, no custodian risk. Pure code.

How it works

1

Deploy your switch

Set your beneficiary address and a check-in timeout period (e.g. 30 days). Optionally deposit MON.

2

Check in regularly

Hitting "Check In" resets your timer. As long as you check in before the deadline, your crypto stays yours.

3

If you stop…

If you miss your deadline — whether by death, disappearance, or lost keys — your beneficiary can claim all funds.
